Slow Cooker Scrambled Eggs

Description

Scrambled eggs cooked in the slow cooker can come out soft and creamy. This recipe is an easy way to prepare scrambled eggs without having to stand over the stove.

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, salt, and black pepper together in a large bowl until well combined.
  2. Coat the inside of a slow cooker with butter.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the prepared slow cooker.
  4. Cover and cook on Low, stirring occasionally, until the eggs are set and creamy, about 2 to 3 hours.
  5. Stir in shredded Cheddar cheese until melted and well combined.
  6. Serve the scrambled eggs hot and enjoy!
